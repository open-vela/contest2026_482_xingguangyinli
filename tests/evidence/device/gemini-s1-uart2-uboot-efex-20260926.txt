Gemini-S1 UART2 U-Boot / USB EFEX evidence
Date: 2026-09-26 (Asia/Shanghai)
Status: UBOOT_RUNTIME_PASSED / USB_EFEX_ENUMERATION_PENDING

Test scope
- Host: Apple Silicon macOS.
- Target: Gemini-S1 (Allwinner R528S3).
- UART: board UART2, 1,500,000 baud, 8N1, no flow control.
- Firmware: gemini-s1-living-canvas-63311f0-factory-layout.img.
- Flash request: keep data, verify after write, reboot after completion.
- The operation remained bounded by the FES/SRV readiness gate.

Host-side observations
- FEL identity was detected as 0x00185900.
- The 20,640-byte FES payload was transferred and executed.
- DRAM initialization completed on the first status check after about 1.01 s.
- FES-reported DRAM parameters were applied to the staged U-Boot header.
- The 638,976-byte U-Boot payload was transferred to 0x40a00000 and execution
  was requested successfully.
- The device did not enumerate as an FES/SRV USB device within the bounded
  45-second wait.

UART2 observations
- FES initialized clocks and the board, then completed DRAM initialization.
- DRAM was identified as 128 MiB DDR3 at 792 MHz.
- The built-in DRAM simple test passed.
- U-Boot 2018.07 started and identified the sun8iw20 platform.
- U-Boot received work mode 0x10 (USB product mode).
- The SPI NAND path reached `NB1 : nand phy init ok` and loaded the environment
  from `SUNXI_FLASH`.
- U-Boot entered `run usb efex` and reported `usb init ok`.
- USB control-endpoint handling then reported:

    err: ep0 fifo_count 0 is not 8
    sunxi usb err: read_request failed

- The device repeatedly processed USB address requests but did not complete
  host enumeration as the expected FES/SRV device.
